# 🦋 La Signora - "La Señora" (The Fair Lady)

**La Signora**, cuyo nombre real es **Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter**, fue la Octava Portadora de los Fatui y una de las antagonistas principales de los primeros capítulos de la historia de Genshin Impact. Es la primera Portadora en aparecer en el juego, y su muerte a manos de la Shogun Raiden en Inazuma marcó un punto de inflexión en la narrativa [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023).

## 📜 Origen: La Cantante de Mondstadt

### Una Joven Enamorada

Hace aproximadamente 500 años, Rosalyne era una joven de Mondstadt que amaba cantar en la plaza de la ciudad [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D). Se enamoró de **Rostam**, el subcomandante de los Caballeros de Favonius, conocido como el "Cachorro de Lobo" [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/). Era la única persona que podía aliviar las preocupaciones de Rostam, y él le regaló un reloj de agua especial para que ella pudiera medir el tiempo que pasaría estudiando en Sumeru [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/).

### La Tragedia del Cataclismo

Rosalyne viajó a la **Akademiya de Sumeru** para estudiar, probablemente magia Pyro [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/). Sin embargo, mientras ella estaba fuera, el Cataclismo asoló Teyvat. Cuando regresó a Mondstadt, descubrió que **Rostam había muerto en la batalla** contra los monstruos del Abismo [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/).

## 🔥 La Bruja Carmesí de las Llamas

### El Nacimiento de un Monstruo

Devastada por la pérdida de su amado, Rosalyne decidió que el mundo debía ser purificado. **Quemó su antigua vida y su cuerpo mortal**, utilizando un arte perdido para hacer fluir fuego líquido a través de sus venas [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D). Así nació la **Bruja Carmesí de las Llamas**, una entidad de fuego vivo que recorrió Teyvat quemando monstruos y demonios [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/).

### La Rechazada por el Pueblo

Aunque solo quemaba a las criaturas del Abismo, la gente la temía y la rechazaba. Cuando veían sus llamas a lo lejos, cerraban sus puertas y la ahuyentaban [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D). A ella no le importaba; había aceptado que su camino hacia la esperanza no necesitaba ser comprendido [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788).

## ❄️ El Reclutamiento de los Fatui

### El Pacto con la Tsaritsa

Antes de que el fuego eterno consumiera por completo su corazón, **Pierro**, el Primer Portador, la encontró. Le ofreció el poder de la Tsaritsa para apagar sus llamas, y a cambio, ella se uniría a los Fatui [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D). Rosalyne aceptó, viendo en la Tsaritsa una aliada en su lucha contra los dioses y el Abismo [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D).

### La Máscara del Olvido

Para contener sus llamas, se le otorgó un **Engaño (Delusion) Cryo** que le permitió vivir sin ser consumida [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D). ## 🎭 Papel en la Historia del Juego

### Mondstadt: El Robo de la Gnosis Anemo

La Signora es la primera Portadora que el Viajero encuentra en persona. Al final del Prólogo, tras la purificación de Dvalin, la Signora tiende una emboscada a Venti fuera de la Catedral de Mondstadt. Lo somete con su poder Cryo y le **arranca su Gnosis** [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/).

### Liyue: El Contrato con Rex Lapis

En el Arconte de Liyue, se revela que La Signora era la intermediaria entre **Zhongli (Morax)** y la Tsaritsa. Como parte de su plan para retirarse, Zhongli entregó su Gnosis a La Signora, quien se la llevó a Snezhnaya [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/).

### Inazuma: El Juicio y la Ejecución

La Signora viajó a Inazuma para supervisar la fábrica de Engaños y, sobre todo, para obtener la Gnosis de la Shogun Raiden [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/). Tras descubrirse su implicación en los planes de los Fatui, el Viajero la desafió a un duelo ante el trono de la Shogun [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/).

La Signora aceptó. Durante el combate, se vio superada y liberó el poder de su Engaño, transformándose en la **"Bruja Carmesí de las Cenizas"**, pero aun así fue derrotada [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/). Como dicta la ley del duelo, la Shogun Raiden la ejecutó con su **Musou no Hitotachi**, reduciéndola a cenizas [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D)[](https://screenrant.com/genshin-impacts-la-signora-death-eighth-fatui-harbinger/).

### Una Antiheroína Trágica

La Signora es una figura trágica: una mujer que, consumida por el dolor y la sed de venganza, perdió su humanidad para convertirse en un instrumento de destrucción [](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%BD%97%E8%8E%8E%E7%90%B3%C2%B7%E5%85%8B%E9%B2%81%E5%85%B9%E5%B8%8C%E5%8D%A1%C2%B7%E6%B4%9B%E5%8E%84%E6%B3%95%E7%89%B9/58430047?lemmaFrom=lemma_starMap&fromModule=lemma_starMap&starNodeId=c9fdac1dd237a8d1280ac9f1&lemmaIdFrom=56967023)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D). A pesar de su arrogancia, era respetada por sus colegas, y su muerte fue llorada por la organización [](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora?oldid=1344788)[](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Signora#:~:text=Before%20she%20could%20be%20fully%20devoured%20by%20her%20own%20flames%2C%20Pierro%20found%20her%20and%20decided%20to%20make%20her%20an%20ally.%20She%20was%20given%20a%20Cryo%20Delusion%20to%20suppress%20her%20flames%20and%20became%20known%20as%20La%20Signora%2C%20the%20Eighth%20of%20the%20Eleven%20Fatui%20Harbingers.%5B1%5D%5B13%5D%5B15%5D%5B16%5D).
